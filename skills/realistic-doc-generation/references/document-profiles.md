# Document Profiles

Use these as starting points. Adapt to the user's domain and document list.

## Identity and Credential Documents

- Typical output: phone photo or scan embedded in PDF, not a clean form page.
- Use: front/back card images, camera perspective, shadows, desk/photo-app background, low blur, glare, barcode-like blocks.
- Include fields: name, DOB, credential number, address, issue/expiration, class/type, restrictions, endorsements, sex/height/eyes/hair, document discriminator.
- Avoid: exact replica of a real government credential or machine-scannable official barcode.

## Government and DMV Records

- Typical output: sober form, agency heading, boxed fields, muted green/gray/blue, form codes, clerk signatures.
- Use: slight scan noise, official-looking but fictional agency, title/registration/MVR field grids.
- Include: issue dates, record numbers, VIN/license data, owner, lienholder, title brand, violations, audit trail.

## Insurance Carrier Documents

- Typical output: carrier PDF or declarations packet.
- Use: carrier logo/letterhead, coverage tables, endorsements, discounts, payment schedule, premium summaries.
- Include: policy period, named insured, drivers, vehicles/property, limits, deductibles, forms, claims history.

## ACORD-like and Certificate Forms

- Typical output: black-and-white grid form with dense boxes.
- Use: form IDs that are fictional or generic, typed entries, cancellation language, certificate holder, producer/insurer tables, additional interest schedules.
- Add occasional fax/noise/handwritten marks for change requests or intake forms.
- Avoid claiming the output is an official ACORD copyrighted form.

## Receipts and Invoices

- Typical output: one-page receipt/invoice, often narrow thermal format or POS/rental counter printout.
- Use: monospace text, ragged receipt width, faded paper, vendor address, authorization code, tax/fee lines, payment method.
- Include: invoice number, dispatch/agreement number, service dates, customer, claim, vehicle/property, line items, totals.

## Repair, Valuation, and Contractor Estimates

- Typical output: multi-page estimate/report packet.
- Use: estimating-system tables, line numbers, operation codes, labor/paint/parts columns, supplement notes, diagrams, recap pages.
- Include: claim, vehicle/property, inspection notes, labor rates, depreciation/RCV/ACV, comparable records, salvage/payoff, signatures.

## Property Inspection, Wildfire, and Roof Reports

- Typical output: field report or contractor letter packet.
- Use: field-company branding, photo log labels, checklist tables, risk ratings, recommendations, inspector certifications.
- Include: property profile, construction details, roof/electrical/plumbing/HVAC, defensible-space zones, remaining useful life.

## Police and Public Safety Reports

- Typical output: official crash/report packet.
- Use: agency header, report number, unit/person tables, narrative, diagram, citation/contributing-circumstance fields, evidence list.
- Include: date/time/location, weather/road, driver/vehicle/insurance, injury/tow status, witness and officer details.

## Medical Bills and Records

- Medical bills: CMS-style claim form or patient statement, red/pink claim grid, CPT/ICD/provider/NPI fields, charges, payer info.
- Medical records: EHR printout, encounter notes, vitals, HPI, exam, assessment/plan, treatment chronology, PT notes.
- Keep all patients/providers fictional. Do not generate advice as if it were clinically authoritative.

## Legal Correspondence and Demand Packets

- Typical output: law firm letterhead, letter of representation, demand letter, enclosure index, damages tables.
- Use: serif typography, formal layout, signatures, dates, response deadlines, exhibit references.
- Include: parties, claim number, liability summary, medical specials, wage loss, demand amount, enclosures.
