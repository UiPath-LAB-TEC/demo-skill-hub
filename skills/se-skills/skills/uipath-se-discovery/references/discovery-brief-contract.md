# Discovery Brief Contract

Produce a concise brief with these sections.

## Summary

- Customer or account.
- Division or team.
- Process.
- One-sentence business problem.
- Source artifacts reviewed.

## Current State

- Current-state snapshot: actors, channels, systems, documents, steps, exceptions, controls, metrics, and bottlenecks.
- Process stages.
- Actors.
- Systems.
- Inputs and outputs.
- Decisions and exceptions.

## Operational Metrics

List captured facts before unresolved questions.

| Metric | Value | Unit | Frequency | Source phrase | Confidence | Remaining question |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Pain And Impact

- Customer-stated pains.
- Operational impact.
- Known metrics.
- Directional or assumed metrics.
- Keep as-is description, pain, impact, and expected benefits as separate ideas. Do not reuse the same paragraph across these fields.

## UiPath Opportunity Map

| Business challenge | Candidate capability | Evidence | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|

Use "Specialized AI Agents" for reasoning, summarization, policy interpretation, prioritization, recommendations, document understanding, or communication understanding.

## Strategic Value Themes

List 3 concise themes that connect captured pains and metrics to business value.

## Demo-Worthy Moments

List scenes where UiPath can visibly change the work: document intake, agent recommendation, system lookup, human review, orchestration branch, outbound communication, audit trail, or completed transaction.

## Open Questions

List questions that materially affect the solution, demo branch, data contract, or validation plan.

Do not ask for a metric already captured in the Operational Metrics section. Ask only for missing, ambiguous, or validation-needed values.
