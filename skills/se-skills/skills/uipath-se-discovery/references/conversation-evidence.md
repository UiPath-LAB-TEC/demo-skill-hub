# Conversation Evidence

Use this guide when extracting facts from transcripts, notes, or email threads.

## Evidence Types

- Direct fact: stated by the customer or visible in an artifact.
- Inference: likely conclusion from multiple facts.
- Assumption: useful placeholder that needs validation.
- Open question: missing information that changes design or demo scope.

## Extraction Rules

- Preserve customer terminology for roles, queues, statuses, documents, and systems.
- Capture exact metrics when stated, including units and time periods.
- Extract metrics before summarizing the process. Look for volume, frequency, staffing, SLA or KPI targets, AHT, backlog, exception or rework, quality, risk, revenue, and savings.
- Treat captured metrics as known evidence. Do not ask for a value that the source already stated; ask only for missing, ambiguous, or validation-needed metrics.
- Capture source wording or a close source phrase for every important metric.
- Record contradictions instead of smoothing them over.
- Keep quotes short and only when they anchor a key pain, metric, or requirement.
- Avoid putting PHI, PII, credentials, or proprietary identifiers into reusable examples.

## Operational Metrics Table

| Metric | Value | Unit | Frequency | Source phrase | Confidence | Remaining question |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stated operational metric | Number, range, target, or qualitative signal | Faxes, referrals, people, minutes, percent, dollars, etc. | Daily, weekly, monthly, per transaction, or blank | Short quote or close paraphrase | High/Medium/Low/Estimate | Only if unresolved |

## Minimum Evidence Table

| Claim | Evidence | Source | Confidence | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Process fact, pain, metric, or rule | Short quote or paraphrase | Transcript section, note, file, or screenshot | High/Medium/Low | Question or validation step |
