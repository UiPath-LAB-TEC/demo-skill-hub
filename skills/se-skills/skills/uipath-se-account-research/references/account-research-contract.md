# Account Research Contract

Produce a concise account brief with citations.

## Sections

- Account snapshot.
- Relevant division, business unit, or operation.
- Process area and why it matters.
- Best-fit process classification, marked as sourced or assumed.
- External signals: initiatives, public strategy, operating pressures, partnerships, product lines, or regulatory context.
- Industry perspective relevant to the process area.
- Likely systems, channels, documents, and data sources.
- Business terminology.
- Operational pain hypotheses.
- Likely operational metrics to validate.
- Strategic value themes.
- UiPath relevance map.
- Discovery questions.
- Demo hooks.
- Sources.

## Fact And Hypothesis Split

| Item | Type | Source or rationale | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sourced fact, inference, or assumption | Sourced fact / inference / assumption | Citation, customer evidence, or rationale | High/Medium/Low |

## Likely Operational Metrics To Validate

Do not invent internal customer values. List metrics the SE should validate, such as volume, frequency, staffing, SLA or KPI targets, AHT, backlog, exception or rework, quality, risk, revenue, and savings.

| Metric to validate | Why it matters | Likely source or owner | Research basis | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Strategic Value Themes

List 3 concise themes that connect public account or industry signals to likely business value. Keep these separate from pain hypotheses and expected benefits.

## UiPath Relevance Map

| Account/process signal | UiPath capability | Demo angle | Source or assumption |
|---|---|---|---|

Use "Specialized AI Agents" for reasoning, summarization, policy interpretation, prioritization, recommendations, document understanding, or communication understanding.

## Discovery Questions

Write questions that validate or disprove research-based hypotheses. Prefer questions that reveal process shape, volume, exception paths, systems, document types, and decision ownership.

Captured customer facts, if any were supplied with the prompt, should appear before questions. Do not ask for values already stated by the customer; ask only for missing, ambiguous, or validation-needed metrics.
