# Slide Story Patterns

Use this structure for concise SE demo decks.

## Discovery Readout Deck

1. Customer context and process scope.
2. Current-state process and pain.
3. Automation opportunity map.
4. UiPath future-state concept.
5. Open questions and next steps.

## Demo Setup Deck

1. Business challenge.
2. Scenario being demonstrated.
3. UiPath capability map.
4. Demo storyline.
5. What is real, mocked, and next.

## Post-Demo Recap Deck

1. What we understood.
2. What we demonstrated.
3. Capability-to-pain mapping.
4. Modeled outcomes and assumptions.
5. Recommended next steps.

## Design Rules

- Lead with the business problem, not the product architecture.
- Keep implementation details in the appendix unless the audience is technical.
- Label directional estimates.
- Include presenter notes when another SE will deliver the story.
